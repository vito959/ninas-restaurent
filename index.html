A table with nowhere else to be.

Nina's opened in 2019 on a ridge where the mountain drops straight into the sea. Every table looks west. The kitchen changes with what the coastal boats and the terrace garden bring in that week, and dinner is never rushed — most evenings run three hours, start to last course.
